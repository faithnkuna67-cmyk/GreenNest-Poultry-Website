About us

A local poultry business built around quality and service.

GreenNest Poultry Supplies is a hypothetical small business created for the WEDS020POE website development project. The organisation focuses on making fresh poultry products accessible to households and local businesses.

Our mission

To provide reliable poultry products and helpful customer service while building long-term relationships with the local community.

Our vision

To become a trusted local supplier with a professional online presence and a reputation for dependable service.

Our values

  • Quality and freshness
  • Reliable customer service
  • Clear communication
  • Respect for customers and the community
